# Vedic Heritage Platform — Phase 4 Rights Re-Audit Report
**Audit Date:** 2026-11-04  
**Auditor:** Principal Software Architect & Lead Legal Compliance Officer  
**Scope:** Rigorous, evidence-based copyright and edition re-audit for all eight completed pilot compositions across multiple legal jurisdictions.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Legal Principles

The re-audit was conducted in strict adherence to the project's foundational principle: **ancient compositions and specific printed critical editions have distinct intellectual property profiles**. A 3,000-year-old Vedic hymn is timelessly in the public domain, but a 20th-century critical edition, typographic layout, editorial commentary, or translation may retain active copyright protection.

### Jurisdictional Framework Applied:
1. **India (Copyright Act, 1957, s.22 & s.24)**:
   - Published literary works: Life of the author/editor + 60 calendar years from the year following death.
   - Government or institutional works (s.28A): 60 years from publication.
2. **United States (17 U.S.C. § 304)**:
   - Works published before January 1, 1930: In the universal public domain.
   - Works published between 1930 and 1977 without copyright notice or unrenewed: In the public domain.
3. **European Union & United Kingdom (Directive 2006/116/EC)**:
   - Author's life + 70 years from the end of the calendar year of death.
4. **Berne Convention Rule of the Shorter Term (Article 7(8))**:
   - Protection in a foreign country will not exceed the term granted in the country of origin.

## 2. Evidence-Based Audit of the 8 Pilot Compositions

### 1. Mahamrityunjaya Mantra (`VEDA-TXT-00005`)
- **Underlying Composition**: Rigveda Samhita (7.59.12), Shukla Yajurveda (3.60), Taittiriya Samhita (1.8.6.i). Revealed to Sage Maitravaruni Vasishtha / Markandeya. **Status: Universal Public Domain.**
- **Edition 1 (VSM Poona, 1933)**:
  - *Editors*: N.S. Sontakke (d. 1978), C.G. Kashikar (d. 2007).
  - *Indian Law (s.22)*: Kashikar died in 2007; 2007 + 60 = **December 31, 2067**. The modern editorial and typographic arrangement of VSM 1933 is **NOT** out of copyright in India for the editors' proprietary notes or arrangement.
  - *Action*: VSM 1933 edition status is returned to **AMBER** for commercial reproduction of its editorial apparatus.
- **Edition 2 (F. Max Müller / Oxford, 1890)**:
  - *Editor*: Friedrich Max Müller (1823–1900). Died 1900.
  - *India (s.22)*: 1900 + 60 = 1960.
  - *US (17 USC 304)*: Published 1890 (pre-1930).
  - *EU/UK*: 1900 + 70 = 1970.
  - *Clearance*: **GREEN — Universal Public Domain**.
  - *Determination*: The platform's text is anchored directly to the 1890 Max Müller edition and 1890 Nirnaya Sagar Press Samhita text. Permitted territories: **GLOBAL (ALL CHANNELS)**.

### 2. Gayatri Vedic Mantra (`VEDA-TXT-00006`)
- **Underlying Composition**: Rigveda Samhita (3.62.10). Revealed to Brahmarshi Vishvamitra. **Status: Universal Public Domain.**
- **Primary Historical Edition**: *Rig-Veda-Sanhita: The Sacred Hymns of the Brahmans*, F. Max Müller, Oxford University Press / W.H. Allen (1890).
  - *Bibliographic Details*: Published in London / Oxford, 1890.
  - *Editor*: F. Max Müller (d. October 28, 1900).
  - *Jurisdictional Status*:
    - India: Public domain since 1961.
    - US: Public domain since 1965 (and under pre-1930 rule).
    - UK/EU: Public domain since 1971.
  - *Clearance*: **GREEN — Universal Public Domain**. Permitted territories: **GLOBAL (COMMERCIAL & DIGITAL)**.

### 3. Sri Suktam (`VEDA-TXT-00007`)
- **Underlying Composition**: Rigveda Khilani (5.87). Ancient Vedic Khila-sukta. **Status: Universal Public Domain.**
- **Edition 1 (Dr. Isidor Scheftelowitz, Breslau, 1906)**:
  - *Full Title*: *Die Apokryphen des Rgveda (Khilani)*, Indische Forschungen, Heft 1, Verlag von M. & H. Marcus, Breslau, 1906.
  - *Editor*: Dr. Isidor Scheftelowitz (1875–1934). Died December 1934 in Oxford, UK.
  - *Jurisdictional Status*:
    - India: 1934 + 60 = December 31, 1994 (Public Domain).
    - US: Published 1906 (pre-1930 rule, Public Domain).
    - UK/EU: 1934 + 70 = December 31, 2004 (Public Domain since Jan 1, 2005).
  - *Clearance*: **GREEN — Universal Public Domain**.
- **Edition 2 (VSM Khila Sangraha, Poona, 1946)**:
  - *Editors*: VSM Editorial Committee (editors survived past 1970).
  - *Action*: VSM 1946 is classified **AMBER**. The platform does **not** rely on VSM 1946 for commercial rights; all 16 verses are sourced exclusively from the Scheftelowitz 1906 critical edition.
