## 2. In-Depth Philological Dossiers for Batch 2 Ingestion Candidates

### Candidate 1: Venkateswara Suprabhatam (`VEDA-TXT-00013`)
- **Deity:** Lord Venkateswara (Sri Srinivasa / Balaji of Tirumala).
- **Composer:** Sri Prativadi Bhayankaram Anna (also known as Sri Annan, born c. 1361 CE), illustrious disciple of Sri Manavala Mamunigal in the Sri Vaishnava lineage.
- **Scripture & Liturgical Use:** Recited daily at 3:00 AM as the opening morning awakening service in the sanctum sanctorum of the Venkateswara Temple at Tirumala.
- **Counting Convention:** The complete morning cycle comprises 70 verses across 4 sections:
  1. Suprabhatam (29 verses)
  2. Stotram (11 verses)
  3. Prapatti (16 verses)
  4. Mangalasasanam (14 verses)
  Our master text canonical record establishes the **Suprabhatam proper as exactly 29 verses**, commencing with *kausalyā suprajā rāma pūrvā sandhyā pravartatē* and concluding with *śrīveṅkaṭācalapatē tava suprabhātam*.
- **Critical Printed Edition:** *Sri Venkateswara Suprabhata Stotram*, Vavilla Ramaswamy Sastrulu & Sons, Madras (1918) and TTD Archival Compendium (1928). Universal public domain (>95 years old).

### Candidate 2: Purusha Suktam (`VEDA-TXT-00014`)
- **Deity:** Purusha / Narayana (The Cosmic Divine Person).
- **Composer:** Rishi Narayana.
- **Scripture:** *Rigveda Samhita* (Mandala 10, Sukta 90); recensions also found in *Shukla Yajurveda* (Vajasaneyi Madhyandina Samhita 31) and *Krishna Yajurveda* (*Taittiriya Aranyaka* 3.12).
- **Counting Convention:** Rigvedic primary critical recension contains exactly **16 Riks**. Verses 1–15 are composed in the Anushtubh metre (4x8=32 syllables); Verse 16 (*yajñēna yajñamayajanta dēvāḥ*) is composed in the Trishtubh metre (4x11=44 syllables). (The 24-verse extended recension in the Vajasaneyi Samhita is cataloged as a distinct secondary recension).
- **Vedic Accents:** Full Vedic musical swaras (Udatta, Anudatta, Svarita, Dirgha Svarita) are mandatory and preserved.
- **Critical Printed Edition:** *Rig-Veda-Sanhita with the Commentary of Sayanacharya*, edited by F. Max Müller, Oxford University Press (1890); Vaidika Samsodhana Mandala critical reprint (1933).

### Candidate 3: Durga Suktam (`VEDA-TXT-00015`)
- **Deity:** Goddess Durga (Agni in the form of Durga, Deliverer from all Afflictions).
- **Composer:** Sage Jatavedas.
- **Scripture:** *Taittiriya Aranyaka*, Prapathaka 10, Anuvakas 1–2 (*Mahanarayana Upanishad* 2.1–7).
- **Counting Convention:** Exactly **7 Riks**. Opening with the celebrated Rigvedic verse *jātavēdasē sunavāma sōmam* (RV 1.99.1) and invoking Agni to convey the soul across all tribulations as a celestial ship over the roaring ocean (*durgāṇi viśvā nāvēva sindhum*).
- **Vedic Accents:** Full Krishna Yajurveda Taittiriya musical accents.
- **Critical Printed Edition:** *The Mahanarayana-Upanishad of the Atharva-Veda with Commentary*, Anandashrama Sanskrit Series No. 27, Poona (1894). Universal public domain (>130 years old).
