## 1. Prioritization Criteria & Governance Matrix

In accordance with Phase 5 requirements, candidate compositions from the 77-entry catalog were scored against seven rigorous criteria:
1. **Religious & Traditional Importance**: Canonically foundational text in Vedic, Puranic, or Smartha traditions.
2. **Popularity & Devotional Usage**: High frequency of daily recitation, temple worship, or festival observance globally.
3. **Availability of Reliable Historical Editions**: Verified physical prints published before 1920 (e.g. Nirnaya Sagar Press, Sri Vani Vilas Press, Chowkhamba).
4. **Ability to Verify Complete Original Text**: Unambiguous, attested verse sequence without missing stanzas or corrupted readings.
5. **Availability of Commercial Usage Rights**: Definitively out-of-copyright across India, US, and UK/EU under public-domain statutes.
6. **Relevance to Future Mobile Application**: High demand for daily devotional features (e.g. morning chanting, daily prayer widgets, festival reminders).
7. **Suitability for Chanting Audio**: Metric regularity, standard melodic recitative structures, and suitability for high-fidelity audio production.
