## 2. Mathematical & Database Resolution of Verse-Count Discrepancy

### The Discrepancy
The previous dashboard report showed `total_verified_verses: 153`.
However:
- 8 Pilot Compositions: 6 + 16 + 31 + 9 + 1 + 1 + 16 + 43 = **123 recorded units**.
- 3 Batch 1 Compositions: 10 + 9 + 8 = **27 recorded units**.
- **Expected Verified Total:** 123 + 27 = **150 recorded units**.
- **Difference:** +3 units.

### Forensic Root Cause
Querying the raw SQLite `verses` and `texts` tables directly revealed:
```sql
SELECT texts.id, texts.content_id, texts.canonical_title, texts.textual_verification_status, COUNT(verses.id)
FROM texts
JOIN verses ON verses.text_id = texts.id
GROUP BY texts.id;
```

Results:
1. `VEDA-TXT-00001` (Ganesha Pancharatnam): 6 verses (`SCHOLAR_VERIFIED`)
2. `VEDA-TXT-00002` (Shiva Tandava Stotram): 16 verses (`SCHOLAR_VERIFIED`)
3. `VEDA-TXT-00003` (Aditya Hrudayam): 31 verses (`SCHOLAR_VERIFIED`)
4. `VEDA-TXT-00004` (Madhurashtakam): 9 verses (`SCHOLAR_VERIFIED`)
5. `VEDA-TXT-00005` (Mahamrityunjaya Mantra): 1 verse (`SCHOLAR_VERIFIED`)
6. `VEDA-TXT-00006` (Gayatri Mantra): 1 verse (`SCHOLAR_VERIFIED`)
7. `VEDA-TXT-00007` (Sri Suktam): 16 verses (`SCHOLAR_VERIFIED`)
8. `VEDA-TXT-00008` (Hanuman Chalisa): 43 verses (`SCHOLAR_VERIFIED`)
9. `VEDA-TXT-00010` (Mahalakshmi Ashtakam): 10 verses (`SCHOLAR_VERIFIED`)
10. `VEDA-TXT-00011` (Lingashtakam): 9 verses (`SCHOLAR_VERIFIED`)
11. `VEDA-TXT-00012` (Sankata Nashana Ganesha Stotram): 8 verses (`SCHOLAR_VERIFIED`)
**Verified Subtotal:** exactly **150 verses**.

The +3 extra verses came from two unverified prototype records created during Phase 1/2 integration testing:
- `VEDA-TXT-00009` (*Test Shiva Stuti 1*): **1 verse** (`TextualVerificationStatus.UNVERIFIED`, `PublicationStatus.DRAFT`)
- `VEDA-TXT-00099` (*Aditya Hrudayam Sample*): **2 verses** (`TextualVerificationStatus.UNVERIFIED`, `PublicationStatus.DRAFT`)
**Prototype Subtotal:** **3 verses**.
**Total Raw Rows in Table:** 150 + 3 = **153 verses**.

### Dashboard Fix Applied
In `automation/reporting/library_analytics.py`:
- Previously, `total_verses += len(t.verses)` naively accumulated all verses across all texts in the database.
- The reporting logic now strictly counts `total_verified_verses` only for texts having `t.textual_verification_status == TextualVerificationStatus.SCHOLAR_VERIFIED` and `len(t.verses) == t.verse_count`.
- The dashboard metrics now report:
  - `total_registered_database_texts`: **13**
  - `verified_complete_texts`: **11**
  - `sample_or_prototype_texts`: **2**
  - `total_verified_verses`: **150**
  - `translation_untranslated_verses`: **3** (reflecting the 3 untranslated verses in the prototype records)
