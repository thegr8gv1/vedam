## 2. Metrical Structure & Verse Reconciliation

- **Metrical Architecture**:
  1. **Opening Dohas (2 Dohas)**:
     - Doha 1: `श्रीगुरु चरन सरोज रज निज मनु मुकुरु सुधारि । बरनउँ रघुबर बिमल जसु जो दायकु फल चारि ॥` (13-11 Matras).
     - Doha 2: `बुद्धिहीन तनु जानिके सुमिरौ पवन-कुमार । बल बुधि बिद्या देहु मोहिं हरहु कलेस बिकार ॥` (13-11 Matras).
  2. **Body Chaupais (40 Chaupais)**:
     - 40 quadrupeds with 16 Matras per hemistich (carana), rhyming in pairs.
     - Enumerates Hanuman's virtues, deeds in the Ramayana, leap to Lanka, bringing Sanjivani, overcoming Ahiravana, and boon-bestowal.
  3. **Closing Doha (1 Doha)**:
     - Doha 3: `पवनतनय संकट हरन मंगल मूरति रूप । राम लखन सीता सहित हृदय बसहु सुर भूप ॥`
- **Verse Count Reconciliation**:
  - 2 Opening Dohas + 40 Chaupais + 1 Closing Doha = **43 recorded units**.
  - Commonly termed *Chalisa* due to the 40 Chaupais in the body, while the 3 Dohas serve as structural invocatory and benedictory frames.
  - In our database, each unit is distinctly tagged:
    - Units 1 & 2: `VerseType.DOHA`
    - Units 3–42: `VerseType.CHAUPAI` (Chaupais 1 to 40)
    - Unit 43: `VerseType.DOHA`
- **Textual Variations**:
  - Chaupai 38: `जो यह पढ़ै हनुमान चालीसा` vs. `जो यह पढ़ै हनुमान चलिसा`.
  - Nawal Kishore 1874 standard text preserved immutably.
