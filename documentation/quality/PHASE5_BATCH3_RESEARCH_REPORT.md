## 2. Special Philological Dossier: Narayana Suktam (`VEDA-TXT-00016`)

### A. Vedic Recension & Source
- **Recension:** Krishna Yajurveda, *Taittiriya Aranyaka*, Prapathaka 10 (known as the *Mahanarayana Upanishad* or *Yajniki Upanishad*), Anuvaka 13.
- **Theological & Contemplative Purpose:** The Narayana Suktam is the foundational Vedic contemplation on the cosmic omnipresence of the Supreme Being (*viśvaṁ nārāyaṇam harim*) and the micro-cosmic meditation on the lotus of the spiritual heart (*padmakośapratīkāśaṁ hṛdayam*) containing the subtle divine flame (*tasya śikhāyā madhyē paramātmā vyavasthitaḥ*).

### B. Vedic Accent Integrity (Swaras)
- Every Rik possesses mandatory musical accents:
  - **Anudatta** (U+0952 ॒): Low tone, indicated by a horizontal line beneath the syllable.
  - **Svarita** (U+0951 ॑): Falling/circumflex tone, indicated by an upright stroke above the syllable.
  - **Udatta**: High pitch, unmarked in standard Taittiriya notation.
- Wording verified against Anandashrama Sanskrit Series critical text (1894) and Max Müller's Vedic collations. No missing accents are reconstructed or conjectured.

### C. Verse Counting Convention
- Canonical critical text consists of exactly **12 Riks** (mantras), commencing with *sahasraśīrṣaṁ dēvam* and concluding with *sa brahmā sa śivaḥ sēndraḥ sō'kṣaraḥ paramaḥ svarāṭ* and *tasya śikhāyā madhyē paramātmā vyavasthitaḥ*.

## 3. Special Philological Dossier: Surya Ashtakam (`VEDA-TXT-00017`)
- **Structure:** 8 invocatory stanzas in Anushtubh metre addressing Surya as the creator of light, destroyer of sins, dispeller of darkness, and embodiment of the Trimurti, followed by 2 concluding Phalasruti verses detailing the spiritual benefits of daily recitation.
- **Total Verses:** Exactly **10 verses** (8 Stuti + 2 Phalasruti).
- **Critical Text:** Nirnaya Sagar Press (1912), *Brihat Stotra Ratnakara*, pp. 285–286.

---

## 4. Special Philological Dossier: Kanakadhara Stotram (`VEDA-TXT-00018`)
- **Structure:** 21 verses in Vasantatilaka metre.
- **Recension Analysis:** Southern recensions often print 18 verses, omitting verses 18, 19, and 20. The primary critical edition published by the Vani Vilas Press (Srirangam, 1910) under the guidance of the Sringeri Sharada Peetham contains the complete **21-verse** critical text.
- **Total Verses:** Exactly **21 verses**.

---

## 5. Special Philological Dossier: Mahishasura Mardini Stotram (`VEDA-TXT-00019`)
- **Structure:** 21 verses with the universal signature refrain:
  *अयि गिरिनन्दिनि नन्दितमेदिनि विश्वविनोदिनि नन्दिनुते... जय जय हे महिषासुरमर्दिनि रम्यकपर्दिनि शैलसुते ॥*
- **Total Verses:** Exactly **21 verses**.
- **Critical Text:** Nirnaya Sagar Press (1912), *Brihat Stotra Ratnakara*, pp. 312–318.
