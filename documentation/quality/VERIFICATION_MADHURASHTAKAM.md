## 2. Metrical Structure & Verse Reconciliation

- **Metrical Pattern**: Totaka meter (तोटक छन्दः — four saganas: `स-स-स-स`, 12 syllables per carana, 48 syllables per stanza).
- **Thematic Construction**:
  - Every single attribute of Sri Krishna is described as sweet (*madhura*), ending with the signature refrain: `मधुराधिपतेरखिलं मधुरम्` ("Everything pertaining to the Lord of Sweetness is utterly sweet").
- **Verse Breakdown (9 Recorded Units)**:
  - **Verse 1**: Lips, face, eyes, smile, heart, gait.
  - **Verse 2**: Words, character, attire, posture, wandering, wandering-about.
  - **Verse 3**: Flute, dust of feet, lotus hands, feet, dance, fellowship.
  - **Verse 4**: Song, drinking, eating, sleeping, form, ornament-tilaka.
  - **Verse 5**: Deeds, swimming/sport in Yamuna, stealing of butter/garments, fulfillment, praise, peace.
  - **Verse 6**: Gunja necklace, flower garland, Yamuna river, ripples, water, lotus.
  - **Verse 7**: Gopis, lila, union, liberation, glance, etiquette.
  - **Verse 8**: Gopas, cows, herd-staff, creation, destruction, fruit of all endeavors.
  - **Verse 9 (`PHALASRUTI`)**: Phalasruti concluding verse in Totaka meter (`श्रीमधुराष्टकमेतन्नित्यं यः पठेत्...`).
- **Verse Count Reconciliation**:
  - Exactly 8 devotional stanzas + 1 concluding phalasruti shloka = **9 units**.
