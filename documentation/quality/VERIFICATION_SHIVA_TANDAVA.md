## 2. Metrical Structure & Verse Reconciliation

- **Metrical Pattern**: Panchachamara meter (पञ्चचामर छन्दः — laghu-guru alternation: `ज-र-ज-र-ज-ग`, 16 syllables per quarter foot, 64 syllables per stanza).
- **Verse Breakdown**:
  - Verses 1–14: Stotra proper describing Shiva's cosmic dance, damaru rhythm, crescent moon, Ganges cascade, garland of serpents, and fire of third eye.
  - Verse 15: Primary Phalasruti shloka in Jagati/Vasantatilaka meter (`इमं हि नित्यमेवमुक्तमुत्तमोत्तमं स्तवं...`).
  - Verse 16: Concluding benediction during Pradosha puja (`पूजावसानसमये दशवक्त्रगीतं... यः शम्भुपूजनपरं पठति प्रदोषे...`).
- **Verse Count Reconciliation**:
  - Some popular collections combine Verses 15 and 16 into a single phalasruti section or label the text as 15 verses.
  - In Nirnaya Sagar Press (1912) and Venkateshwara Steam Press (1908), Verses 15 and 16 are distinct independent shlokas, yielding **16 verses total**.
  - **Resolution**: Ingest all 16 distinct verses with verses 15 and 16 tagged as `PHALASRUTI`.
- **Textual Variations**:
  - Verse 12 line 1: `कदा निलिम्पनिर्झरीनिकेतनोत्तरे वसन्` vs. `कदा निलिम्पनिर्झरीपरिसरे वसन्`. Nirnaya Sagar 1912 standard adopted.
