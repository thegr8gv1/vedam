### Our Project Vision & Purpose
We are building a permanent, scholarly digital infrastructure dedicated to preserving and making accessible India's Vedic and devotional heritage. Our long-term mission encompasses:
1. An open, scholarly text repository supporting all 22 Eighth Schedule Indian languages, Sanskrit, and English.
2. Production-grade chanting audio tools (supporting traditional Japa repetitions of 1, 3, 11, 21, 27, 54, 108 counts).
3. A globally accessible mobile application (iOS & Android) with an astronomical location-aware Vedic calendar, daily ritual Sankalpam generation, and offline recitation capabilities.
4. Professional publication-grade exports in Word, Excel, and JSON formats for teachers, students, and ashrams.

### Exploring a Collaborative Partnership (Track A)
Rather than executing automated web crawling or relying on fragmented downloads, our founding principle is **scholarly integrity, legal transparency, and mutual collaboration**.

We would be deeply honored to explore a formal partnership with Vaidika Vignanam around the following areas:

1. **Structured Data Access:** Exploring authorized, structured database access or JSON/CSV exports of text collections, eliminating any server load or scraping overhead on `vignanam.org`.
2. **Commercial & Derivative Rights:** Establishing a transparent agreement regarding commercial mobile application distribution, audio production, and print exports, accompanied by appropriate licensing terms or royalties/grants.
3. **Prominent Attribution & Integration:** Featuring Vaidika Vignanam as a foundational partner across our mobile apps, publications, and digital portals, with direct attribution badges and inbound links.
4. **Bidirectional Editorial Exchange:** Establishing a scholarly feedback loop where verified textual corrections, additional recensions, and cross-referenced variant readings identified by our editorial team are shared directly with Vaidika Vignanam.
5. **Vedic Accent & Script Orthography Synchronization:** Collaborating on standardized Unicode combining character standards for Vedic swaras across South Indian and North Indian scripts.
