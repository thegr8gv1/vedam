Literal Translation Col 3

View Status: DRAFT (v1)
Source: Padachheda & Grammatical Word Dissection
With His neck purified by the torrential streams of the celestial river Ganges cascading from the dense forest of His matted locks, wearing the lofty serpent hanging down like a garland, and with His sacred Damaru sounding damad-damad-damad-damat, Lord Shiva performed the fierce cosmic Tandava dance; may that Shiva extend all auspiciousness unto us!
Padachheda Analysis:

Traditional Tatparya Col 4

View Status: DRAFT (v1)
Commentator Citation: Traditional Shaiva Sampradaya (Attributed to Ravana)
The opening verse sets the cosmic tempo in the Panchachamara metre. The matted hair represents the cosmic forest (Jata-atavi); the descending Ganga signifies the cleansing grace of consciousness descending into manifestation; the Damaru's rhythmic pulse is the primordial sound (Nada Brahman) sparking creation.
