Traditional Tatparya Col 4

View Status: DRAFT (v1)
Commentator Citation: Traditional Sampradaya Commentary
In his commentary on Rigveda 3.62.10, Sayanacharya explains that 'Savitr' is the inner animating Lord of all celestial luminaries who stimulates the cosmic order and inner faculties of all beings (sarvasya prasavitā). 'Bhargas' is interpreted as the supreme light that burns away the darkness of ignorance and sin (avidyā-nāśakam tejah). 'Varenyam' indicates that which is fit to be sought and prayed for by all seekers of liberation. The prayer concludes with a collective plural petition ('nah dhiyah'): may that supreme illuminating consciousness illuminate the intellects of all beings toward truth, virtue, and contemplation of the Supreme Brahman. Bhatta Bhaskara Mishra's commentary on Taittiriya Aranyaka 10.27 further elucidates that the Vyahritis (bhur bhuvas suvah) establish the pervasive presence of this divine light across the tripartite cosmos.
Source Reference: Rigveda Samhita 3.62.10; Sayana Bhashya (VSM, 1933); Taittiriya Aranyaka 10.27 with Bhatta Bhaskara Mishra's Jnana Yajna Bhashya (Govt. Oriental Library, Mysore, 1900).
