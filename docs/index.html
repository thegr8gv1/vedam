Privacy Policy for Vedam

Application: Vedam • Developer: thegr8gv1 • Effective Date: November 10, 2026 • Repository: github.com/thegr8gv1/Vedam
The Vedam Privacy Guarantee Vedam is engineered with a strict 100% offline, on-device architecture. The devotee mobile application does not require an account, has zero tracking or analytics SDKs, and completely omits internet permissions in its release package. Your personal devotional practices, japa counts, gotra selections, and reflections never leave your device.

1. Introduction

Vedam is a sacred Vedic scripture, devotional chanting, and personal sadhana platform developed by thegr8gv1. We recognize that your spiritual contemplation, prayer, japa mala counting, and scripture studies are private and sacred. This Privacy Policy explains how our software operates and details our strict zero-data-collection architecture.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Vedam does not collect, record, process, transmit, or share any user data:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or government IDs.
  • No Device Identifiers or Telemetry: We do not track IMEI numbers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, or MAC addresses.
  • No Financial Details: Vedam is completely free. There are zero in-app purchases, advertisements, or payment processors.
  • No Location Tracking: Vedam does not access your physical GPS coordinates. Temple orientation (DivyaDishi) uses approximate city coordinates explicitly selected by you.

3. On-Device Storage & Local Operation

Every feature in Vedam executes locally on your physical device hardware:

  • Japa Chanting Counts: Repetition totals, bead positions, and daily goals are saved solely in Android SharedPreferences on your phone.
  • Nitya Sadhana Routines: Custom multi-step practice sequences remain securely in your local application database.
  • Vedic Sankalpa Builder: Ancestral gotra and Vedic shakha inputs are processed strictly in temporary device memory to construct liturgical Sanskrit text.
  • Offline Ephemeris & Panchangam: Astronomical calculations (Tithi, Nakshatra, Rahu Kalam) are computed on-device using mathematical algorithms without connecting to remote servers.
  • DivyaDishi Live Compass: Sacred directions and geomagnetic declination are calculated offline using the NOAA World Magnetic Model (WMM-2025) running directly on your phone's processor.

4. Android Permissions & Justifications

The release package for Vedam includes only the minimum permissions necessary for offline devotional utility:

Android Permission Feature Purpose Network Transmission?
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Allows devotional audio chanting to play uninterrupted when the screen is locked or while multitasking. Zero (No Network)
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Complies with Android 14+ background media playback requirements for continuous Japa audio. Zero (No Network)
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays local media controls (Play / Pause / Stop) in the notification drawer and local prayer reminders. Zero (No Network)
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Schedules local prayer, Sandhya, and Ekadashi reminders to ring at your exact designated times. Zero (No Network)
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restores your scheduled local reminders after your phone restarts. Zero (No Network)
VIBRATE Provides subtle haptic feedback on each bead advance during Japa Mala counting. Zero (No Network)
WAKE_LOCK Keeps audio chanting active during screen-off meditation. Zero (No Network)

Internet Access Verification: The android.permission.INTERNET permission is completely excluded from the release APK and Android App Bundle manifests. The application is technically incapable of sending data over the network.

5. Zero Third-Party Telemetry or Advertising

Vedam integrates zero third-party software development kits (SDKs) for tracking or advertising. There is no Google AdMob, Firebase, Meta SDK, Crashlytics, or tracking cookies. All fonts, UI assets, and 31 core audio recordings are bundled directly inside the local application binary.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Because Vedam does not collect any personally identifiable information from any user, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and applicable global child data protection regulations. The app is completely safe and appropriate for users of all ages.

7. User Control & Data Deletion

You have absolute control over your local data. You can delete all practice history and saved preferences at any time by:

  • Selecting Settings → Reset Practice History inside the app; or
  • Opening your device's Android Settings and choosing Apps → Vedam → Clear Storage; or
  • Uninstalling the application from your device, which automatically purges all locally stored data.
